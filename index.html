Netflix Furigana – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Netflix Furigana is a Chrome extension that adds furigana, English subtitles and word lookup to Japanese subtitles on netflix.com. It does not collect, store or sell personal data, and it has no analytics, tracking or ads.

What the extension handles

  • Subtitles. Japanese and English subtitle text is read and processed only in your browser. The Japanese dictionary used for furigana is bundled with the extension. Subtitle files are downloaded from Netflix, the same way the Netflix player downloads them.
  • Word lookup. When you click a word in a subtitle, that single word is sent to the public jisho.org API (https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words) to fetch its dictionary entries. Nothing else is sent: no account details, no viewing history, no identifiers. jisho.org's handling of requests is covered by its own policies. You can turn word lookup off in the extension's popup.
  • Pronunciation. Audio uses Chrome's built-in speech synthesis. If Chrome uses an online voice (such as "Google 日本語"), Chrome sends the spoken reading to Google to generate the audio, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Settings. Your settings (furigana on/off, size, English on/off, lookup on/off) are saved with chrome.storage.sync, so Chrome may sync them between your own signed-in browsers. The developer never receives them.

Data sharing

The developer does not receive any data from the extension, and no data is sold or transferred to third parties for advertising, credit or any other purpose besides the word lookup described above.
